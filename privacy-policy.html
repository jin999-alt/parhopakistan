Privacy Policy

How this website handles information and third-party services.

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy applies to the ParhoPakistan website at https://jin999-alt.github.io/parhopakistan/.

Information we ask you to provide

ParhoPakistan does not currently require visitors to register, create an account or submit personal details to browse the website or open the listed PDF resources. We do not provide a website login or user profile feature.

Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub and the infrastructure providers involved in delivering the website may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request information and security logs. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and terms.

Cookies and analytics

ParhoPakistan does not currently intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies through its own website code. Your browser or hosting provider may process technical data as part of delivering the site.

Advertising

Google AdSense advertising is not currently integrated into this website. If advertising is added in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise advertisements, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and Google's policies. This policy will be updated before advertising is enabled. You can learn more about Google advertising technologies.

External links

Some links open files or services hosted by third parties, including GitHub. We do not control those services. Review their privacy policies before providing them with personal information.

Children and students

This website provides general educational resources. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through public links or messages. Students should ask a parent, guardian or trusted adult for help when a service requests personal or payment information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For a privacy question, use the Contact page. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue.
