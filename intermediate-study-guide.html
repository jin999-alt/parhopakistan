Punjab Intermediate · All Groups

Class 11 and Class 12 study roadmaps for all major Intermediate pathways and all nine Punjab boards.

Important: This is original general study guidance, not an official BISE pairing scheme, model paper, chapter-wise textbook note set or exam prediction. Group names and elective combinations may differ by college and session. Confirm your exact subjects from your registration card and latest official notices.

Choose your group

Each group has separate Class 11 and Class 12 guidance below.

FSc Pre-Medical

Biology, Chemistry, Physics and compulsory subjects (confirm your registered combination).

Class 11 · First Year

Start with the current Class 11 textbook and syllabus. Make a chapter checklist, learn key definitions/formulas, practise short and long questions, and complete practical work where prescribed.

  • Keep a separate notebook for each subject.
  • Practise topic-wise questions before timed papers.
  • Record weak topics and revisit them weekly.

Class 12 · Second Year

Use the current Class 12 syllabus and textbook. Keep revision of Class 11 foundations where they are needed, but do not assume Class 11 and Class 12 paper schemes are identical.

  • Make a subject-wise revision calendar.
  • Attempt timed practice papers and check mistakes.
  • Prepare diagrams, numericals, practicals or written answers according to your subjects.
Subject confirmation: Biology, Chemistry, Physics and compulsory subjects (confirm your registered combination). Always follow your own registration card and current board/PECTAA notifications.

Weekly study routine

Use this routine in both years, adjusting sessions to your actual subjects.

Monday–Tuesday

Study your hardest core subject, then revise one theory or compulsory subject. Finish with 10 short questions.

Wednesday–Thursday

Practise textbook exercises, diagrams, numericals, writing or practical tasks. Review your mistake notebook.

Friday–Sunday

Rotate remaining subjects, attempt a timed practice section, check answers, and plan the next week.

Exam preparation checklist

  • Confirm board, class, group and registered subjects.
  • Use the syllabus and deleted-topic notice for the correct exam session.
  • Keep Class 11 and Class 12 chapter trackers separate.
  • Practise with papers that match your current syllabus and year.
  • Prepare practical records and viva topics where required.
  • In numericals, show formula, substitution and units; in diagrams, label clearly.
