How to contact us

ParhoPakistan currently uses the project's GitHub issue page for public website feedback and correction reports.

Open a contact / issue request

What to include

  • The page or resource link that needs attention.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • For a broken PDF, mention the class and resource title.

Protect your privacy

GitHub issues may be publicly visible. Do not include passwords, phone numbers, home addresses, identity documents, private school records or other sensitive information. If you are a student, ask a parent or trusted adult before sharing personal details online.

Response times

Messages are reviewed when possible. A response or correction is not guaranteed, but clear and specific reports are easier to investigate.
